Alex's Story

From a backyard
to the blockchain

Alexandra "Alex" Scott was diagnosed with neuroblastoma before her first birthday. At just 4 years old, she decided she wanted to help — setting up a lemonade stand to raise money for doctors helping other kids with cancer.

By the time Alex passed away at age 8 in 2004, she had raised over $1 million. Her parents kept her dream alive. Today, Alex's Lemonade Stand Foundation has raised over $250 million for childhood cancer research.

$ALEX brings her mission on-chain. Every buy, every trade, every holder — another drop in the glass she started filling over 20 years ago.
